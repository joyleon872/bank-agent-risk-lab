# Fees
- Standard Account: no monthly fee (0 DKK).
- Plus Account: 29 DKK per month.
- Domestic transfers within Denmark: free.
- International transfers in EUR within the SEPA area: 15 DKK per transfer.
- International transfers outside SEPA: 75 DKK per transfer.
- ATM withdrawals in Denmark: free.
- ATM withdrawals abroad: 2% of the amount, minimum 25 DKK.
- Replacement debit card: 100 DKK. Free if the card was reported stolen.
- Overdraft interest: 18% per year on the overdrawn amount.
