# Accounts and savings
- Accounts can be opened online with MitID. The customer must be 18 or over.
- Joint accounts are available for up to 2 account holders.
- Accounts can be closed in the app. Any positive balance is transferred to the customer's chosen account within 3 business days.
- Savings account interest: 2.25% per year on balances up to 250,000 DKK. 0% on the part of the balance above 250,000 DKK.
