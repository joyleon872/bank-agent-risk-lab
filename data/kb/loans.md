# Personal loans
- Loan amounts: 10,000 DKK to 300,000 DKK.
- Terms: 1 to 10 years.
- Variable interest rate from 6.9% APR, depending on an individual credit assessment.
- Requirements: age 18 or over, Danish residence, stable income, and no registration in RKI (the Danish debtor register).
- Applications are made in the app. A decision is given within 2 business days.
- Early repayment is free of charge.
- Approval is never guaranteed and always depends on the credit assessment.
